Round 3 · States & edge cases
Every card is a true-scale crop of a round-3 component outside the happy path. NEW marks proposals the current app doesn't have.
1

Channel strips · expanded

One strip per feed under its picture. Record and output controls always reflect what the feed can actually do right now.
2

Channel strips · collapsed

Chevron or ⌥⌘S. One line keeps state, elapsed, drops, effects, audio and the record button.
The summary line never hides a problem. Warnings turn the line's text orange, errors red, and the record button disables with the same rule as the expanded strip.
3

Pictures & Program

Tally lives on the feed pictures. No separate Program picture unless you choose Program view.
4

Session sidebar

Problems show in the section they belong to, with one action each.
5

Status bar

Shown at the real 1320 pt window width. Each segment opens a popover with details.
6

Window, menu bar & sheets

First launch, dropdown states, icon states, layout scaling, quit and the ⋯ popover.
