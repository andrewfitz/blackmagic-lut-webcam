Ultracam UI redesign: mockups, round 2

Built on round 1's A · Studio, with C's status bar and C's compact native menu rows added to A's graphical menu bar dropdown. Designed for one or two cameras most of the time, scaling to four. Proposals the current app doesn't have are marked NEW. Round 1

What changed from your feedback

  • No full sidebar just to list 1–2 feeds. V1 turns the sidebar into a session panel (output, recording destination and space left, recent takes, Recover, automation). V2 removes it, so each feed gets a channel. V3 shrinks it to a 64 pt rail.
  • C's status bar is in every variation: virtual camera → who's using it, recording with time left, Program, resources.
  • Hybrid dropdown: A's live preview of the published feed (both feeds when recording two, Program plus cut thumbnails in Program mode), followed by C-style compact rows with checkmarks, shortcuts and per-camera submenus.
  • Side-by-side monitoring with independent controls per feed (V2): each feed has its own record/pause/stop, folder, time left, virtual camera, processing, effects, audio and monitor tools.
  • Recording as backup or standalone: recordings are always the clean 2160p 10-bit source (that's what the app records today), whether or not anything uses the virtual camera.

Real-life use cases walked through

1 · Everyday meeting, one camera (V1)

  • With "Start with camera apps" on, the feed starts when Zoom opens Ultracam. NEW "Record with camera apps" starts a backup take at the same time.
  • A glance at the menu bar dropdown shows what Zoom sees, ON AIR, REC with elapsed time, timecode and audio.
  • LUT on/off or bypass straight from the dropdown (⇧⌘B, Effects submenu). No need to open the window.
  • When Zoom closes, the feed stops and the take finalizes. It shows up under Recent Takes.

2 · Standalone two-camera recording (V2)

  • No virtual camera involved; the status bar says so. Each channel shows its picture with exposure and focus tools.
  • Record All from the toolbar or ⌥⌘R, then pause or stop either camera on its own.
  • Each camera can record to a different drive, with time left per drive and a warning before it runs out NEW.
  • Drops, bitrate and encoder details stay with each channel, so a problem on one camera is obvious.

3 · Two-camera meeting with switching (V3)

  • Zoom gets "Ultracam · Program". The multiview shows Program large and both sources with red/green tally.
  • Cut by clicking a source, pressing ⌃1/⌃2, or clicking the cut thumbnails in the dropdown. Or let Auto run the natural talking-head director.
  • A shot timer and the director timeline NEW show why and when it cuts.
  • Both clean sources are recorded as backup, alongside the cut timeline sidecar, so the meeting can be re-edited.

4 · Setup, problems and recovery (States, Settings)

  • First run: Install Camera inline (or "needs a signed build"). Recording defaults, switcher tuning and the camera extension live in Settings (⌘,).
  • Each problem appears where it happens, with one action: No signal, Unsupported mode, LUT Locate…, Choose folder, Retry, Recover.
  • The menu bar icon shows recording (red dot) and needs-attention (orange dot) NEW, so a backup can be checked without opening anything.
  • Quitting while recording shows exactly what's still being written.

Proposals marked NEW (need model/runtime work)

  • Record with camera apps (start/stop a backup with the virtual camera session); stop when the last app closes.
  • Time left per destination drive, based on codec and number of recording feeds, with low-space warnings.
  • Recent takes list with Reveal in Finder (the Recover flow already exists).
  • A per-feed "Publish as camera" toggle (the virtual camera registry already has an enabled flag per feed).
  • A director timeline view (the data already goes to the timeline sidecar). Menu bar icon recording and attention dots.
  • Shortcuts: ⌃1–⌃4 cut, ⌃A auto, ⌥⌘R record all.

Implementation notes

  • Hybrid dropdown: SwiftUI MenuBarExtra(.menu) can't host video. Two options: an AppKit NSStatusItem + NSMenu whose first item is a custom view with the preview (real menu rows, submenus and keyboard navigation for free; needs a spike to confirm the native video layer renders inside a menu), or keep .window and draw rows that look like a menu (easy video, but submenus and keyboard navigation are hand-built). Recommendation: spike the NSMenu option first.
  • Previews: V2/V3 show 2–3 live pictures plus thumbnails in the dropdown. Today there is one main native preview, one menu bar preview and the monitor surfaces. Extra pictures should use monitor surfaces (which already suspend when hidden), and V1's sidebar thumbnail can be a periodic still.
  • Start/Stop applies to the selected feed in Separate mode and to all sources in Program mode (current behavior). V2's per-channel Stop Feed keeps that model.
