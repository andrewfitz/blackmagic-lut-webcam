2Multiview: Program (what Zoom sees) large, sources beside it with red/green tally. Click a source, or press ⌃1 / ⌃2, to cut.

4NEWDirector timeline: on air and speech activity per camera, with cut reasons. Makes auto-switching readable and matches the sidecar it already writes.
5The inspector follows the rail selection. Program shows output, switching, shot timing and backup recording of both clean sources.
6Dropdown: Program preview, with live camera thumbnails that act as cut buttons, then compact rows for auto, recording, cameras and app.
