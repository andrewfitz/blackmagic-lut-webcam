1The inspector (⌥⌘I, or Show Inspector in a camera's ⋯ menu) holds the deep settings for the selected camera: source, processing and the 4 ordered effects, LUT, recording folder, automation. Closed by default.
2The inspector follows the selection: clicking Camera 2's picture or strip, or pressing ⌘2, switches it. The selected camera keeps its blue ring.
3Stop Camera asks first only when it would end a recording or an output that's in use. The destructive button is red, and Cancel is the default.
4With the inspector open the sidebar is hidden (⌃⌘S) so the two pictures keep a usable width. Both can be open at once; the strips then collapse to one line.
5Effects that change the camera output format say so in place. Changing them is allowed while in use; the strip shows "Updating output…" briefly.
