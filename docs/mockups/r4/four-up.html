1With 3–4 cameras the session sidebar is hidden (⌃⌘S brings it back), giving four 16:9 pictures the full width. Layout follows the camera count: 1 → Single, 2 → Side by Side, 3–4 → Four Up.
2Separate mode: each camera publishes its own "Ultracam · Camera N". Camera 1 has a client, so it gets the red ring and ON AIR. No app name is shown; the app only knows that something is using it.
3Hovering a picture reveals its tool bar (tools, timecode, signal, freeze, clean, zoom, detach). Clicking it selects the camera (blue ring) for ⇧⌘B, ⌘R and the Camera menu.
4Honest empty state: Camera 3 says No HDMI signal and what the input needs. Its strip reads Waiting for HDMI, and its record control is disabled until a signal arrives.
5Strips auto-collapse in Four Up: state, elapsed, effect chips with checkmarks, audio, the record control and the ⋯ camera menu. Paused shows orange bars, never the red tally.
6Status bar: outputs in use, takes (2 recording, 1 paused), time left on each destination drive, and an orange warning segment for Camera 3.
