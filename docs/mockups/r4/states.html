Round 4 · States & edge cases
True-scale crops of round-4 components outside the happy path, built from the shared builders. NEW marks proposals the app doesn't have yet.
1

Channel strips · expanded

One record control per take. Record is enabled only when the camera is live, a folder is set and no take is finishing.
2

Channel strips · collapsed

Chevron or ⌥⌘S. Warnings tint the line orange and errors red, so collapsing never hides a problem.
3

Pictures & Program

Red = ON AIR only. NEXT appears only while a cut is pending. A click selects (blue ring) and never cuts.
4

Session sidebar

Problems show in the section they belong to, with one action each.
5

Status bar

Real 1320 pt width, fixed order: output · Program · recording · audio · problems · resources. The worst state colors its segment.
6

Sheets, menus & the menu bar

Confirmation, locked topology, keep-awake, quit, onboarding, dropdown and icon states.
7

Transitions

How a strip moves between states. Every arrow is driven by a real recorder or capture event.
1Expanded strips use the builder's states: Ready, Recording, Paused, Finishing, Saved, No folder, Failed, Recording · no signal, Stopped, Waiting for HDMI, Updating output, plus bypass and LUT loading.
2Collapsed strips keep the same words and color, plus a record button that disables by the same rule.
3No orange ring for a skipped source (D4): a text badge says why. No dashed "cutting" ring: a pending cut is NEXT. Unplugged input and no HDMI signal have different words.
4Sidebar: first run, install, approval pending, unsigned build, interrupted takes, disk space, armed backup and the automatic-mode line.
5Status bar: normal, recording, warning (no signal while recording), error (drive full) and nothing published. No app names, only "in use".
6Stop Camera asks before ending a take. Rename and Add say why they're locked. The app keeps the Mac awake while cameras run and warns before a lid close.
7Transitions: Ready → Recording → Finishing → Saved; Recording ↔ Recording · no signal; Recording → Failed → Saved up to the last fragment.
